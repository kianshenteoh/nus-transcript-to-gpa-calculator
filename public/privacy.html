About Privacy Methodology

Privacy Policy

This site is designed to process transcript data in the browser. Transcript parsing does not require a dedicated upload backend.

Local storage

The calculator stores your course data locally in your browser so your session can persist between visits on the same device.

Analytics

The site uses Vercel Analytics, Vercel Speed Insights, and Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic and performance. These services may collect standard usage and device information.

Third-party links

This site links to external services such as NUS Registrar pages, myEduRec, NUSMods, Vercel, and GitHub. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Updates

If the site's behaviour changes, this page should be updated to reflect the new data flow.
